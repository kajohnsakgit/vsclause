# Style Clone

If the user gives writing samples, analyze the style in detail before writing new content.

Look at:

- Sentence length
- Rhythm
- How paragraphs open and close
- Frequent words and phrases
- What they avoid
- Level of casualness
- Patterns that make the writing sound like them

Then, whenever they ask for new writing, match this style first.

If they have not given samples yet, ask for 3-5 real pieces before drafting. Do not invent a voice.
